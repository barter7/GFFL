*"It's not whether you get knocked down, it's whether you get up." — Vince Lombardi, as adopted this week by a team named after its injured list*

Welcome back one and all to the eighth weekly installment of the GFFL Recap! The league no longer has a winless team. It does still have an unbeaten one, which beat the league's second-highest-scoring team this week to go 8-0. One team left 60 points on its bench, the most anyone has left there all season, and won anyway. Another set the best lineup it possibly could and lost by two. And the commissioner made every big lineup decision correctly, mostly because the NFL schedule made them for him.

## Injury Reservoir 117.58 — More Like Blo Dix 111.28

Before Thursday's game, Slob onmy Doubs (DoubanteAdams) became **Injury Reservoir**, which is fair: Romeo Doubs, the man in the old name, is on its IR. By Monday night, it had its first win of the season.

With C.J. Stroud on a bye, Kirk Cousins got the start back, and he completed 19 of 26 passes for 247 yards and three touchdowns and ran for a fourth in Las Vegas's 38–33 win over the Jets (28.18). Tee Higgins caught 10 of 11 targets for **155 yards and two touchdowns** in Cincinnati's 43–36 overtime win over Tennessee, the second one tying the game with 1:20 left (32.5). Chase McLaughlin kicked three field goals in Tampa Bay's 16–0 win over Atlanta (12), Chase Brown added 12.1, Saquon Barkley scored in Washington (11.9), and the Bears defense scored 10 on Monday night in Seattle. Kyle Pitts scored 1.6 in that Falcons shutout, and the best lineup on the roster was 130.98, but nobody at Injury Reservoir is complaining about anything this week. 1-7, and the league is out of 0-fers.

More Like Blo Dix had this one on its bench. **Tre Tucker started in the flex and scored zero** in that Raiders win; DeMario Douglas, on the bench, caught three passes for 76 yards and a touchdown from Drake Maye (15.1). That swap alone wins by 8.8. Douglas has now scored 16.1 and 15.1 on this bench in the last three weeks, with a 3.8 in between, which was the week he started. The quarterback question took care of itself: Tyler Shough was on a bye, so Bo Nix started, threw for 222 yards and two touchdowns, and led Denver back from 24–13 down in the fourth quarter to beat Kansas City (19.28). Puka Nacua caught 11 of 12 targets and a touchdown (19.5), Trey McBride caught a touchdown in Arizona's overtime win in Dallas (16.7), and Luther Burden III scored on Monday night (15.1). Bijan Robinson ran 19 times for 76 yards in Atlanta's shutout loss (7.6). 4-4.

## Whistlin' Willy's 88.48 — Chug City 86.4

The closest game of the week (2.08), and one of the strangest. Willy's went into Monday night leading 74.5 to 71.0, with Caleb Williams to play against Chug City's Rome Odunze and Jadarian Price, in the same Bears–Seahawks game. Odunze (10.7) and Price (4.7) outscored Williams (13.98) by 1.42. Willy's survived by 2.08.

It should not have been close, because Willy's left **60.06 points on its bench**, the most any team has left there all season by almost 17. Tyler Allgeier, who started in the flex the last three weeks and scored 2.6, 5.3 and 6.4, went to the bench this week and ran 11 times for 147 yards and a touchdown and caught five passes for 44 yards and another, in Arizona's comeback overtime win in Dallas (**33.6**). Jordan Mason started in the flex and scored 2.0. And after correctly benching Patrick Mahomes last week, Willy's benched him again, and Mahomes threw for 321 yards and three touchdowns in Denver (25.44) while Williams threw for 247 and one. The best lineup on this roster was 148.54, which would have been the second-highest score of the week. Instead, Tucker Kraft caught a touchdown from Jordan Love on Thursday night (14.3), Blake Corum (13.3) and Kyren Williams (12.4) both scored in the Rams' 24–9 win over the Chargers, and the Rams defense added 11. Javonte Williams ran 24 times for 56 yards (6.2). 5-3.

Chug City scored 86.4. Its best possible lineup was 86.4. Last week I set a perfect lineup and lost by 30; this week Chug City did it and lost by 2.08, which is worse. Jaylen Warren ran 17 times for 143 yards and a touchdown in Pittsburgh's 27–6 win over Cleveland (20.8), and Dak Prescott threw a touchdown and ran for another (19.8), though Dallas blew a 17–3 lead and lost in overtime. Jason Sanders kicked four field goals for the Jets (15), and the Dicker Watch, week 6 of the series, goes to the starter: Cameron Dicker kicked three field goals and scored 11 on the bench. Jonathan Taylor scored 4.7 in Indianapolis's 42–7 loss in Jacksonville, and DK Metcalf 0.9. 2-6.

## The Fellowship of the Ringless 159.36 — Darn Tuten 135.18

First place against second place in points, 7-0 against 6-1, and the second-highest score of the week lost by 24.18. But for most of the weekend, it was winning: Darn Tuten finished Sunday ahead 135.18 to 131.46, with nobody left to play. The Fellowship had D'Andre Swift and the Seahawks defense on Monday night. Swift carried 27 times for 69 yards and **three touchdowns** in Chicago's 34–17 win in Seattle (26.9).

That's 8-0 and 1,162.94 points. Tony Pollard ran for 117 yards and two touchdowns in that Tennessee shootout in Cincinnati (27.2), Josh Allen threw two touchdowns and ran for another in Buffalo's 36–10 win over Baltimore (26.56), Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 153 yards and a touchdown against Minnesota (23.8), and Matthew Golden caught a touchdown on Thursday (17.4). With Ka'imi Fairbairn on a bye, Tyler Bass kicked three field goals for Buffalo (16). The one zero in the lineup was Zay Flowers, on a day Baltimore went through three quarterbacks.

Darn Tuten scored 135.18 and lost, which is now an accepted way to lose to this team. Bhayshul Tuten ran for 104 yards and a touchdown and caught two passes for 55 yards and another in Jacksonville's 42–7 rout of the Colts (28.9). Bucky Irving ran 27 times for **170 yards** and a touchdown in Tampa Bay's shutout (21.0). Josh Jacobs ran for one touchdown and caught another on Thursday night (20.2), Christian Watson had 113 receiving yards in the same game (13.3), and Jayden Daniels threw a touchdown in Washington's win over Philadelphia (14.98). CeeDee Lamb (9.3) and Amon-Ra St. Brown (9.1) were quiet, and Michael Wilson caught eight passes for 111 yards and the tying touchdown with 10 seconds left in Dallas from Minshew, on the bench (22.8). Even the best lineup (151.88) loses. Consolation: Darn Tuten is the second team past 1,000 points (1,070.44). 6-2.

## The Irreversible Binomials 107.6 — Man Getting Pissed at Football 93.48

A week after renaming itself away from Jackson Five (and One), this team got Lamar Jackson back from IR and started him. Jackson dropped back once on Baltimore's first possession and didn't play again; Huntley and then Fagnano finished the game. Zero points from the quarterback, and the Binomials won by 14.12 anyway. Ja'Marr Chase caught 11 of 16 targets for 141 yards and a touchdown in Cincinnati's overtime win (25.6). Brock Bowers caught seven passes for 92 yards and two touchdowns from Cousins (24.7). Chuba Hubbard ran for a touchdown and caught seven passes on Thursday night (21.4), and Jameson Williams caught the go-ahead touchdown with 4:40 left in Detroit's 19–15 win over Minnesota (15.0). Ryan Flournoy caught a touchdown on the bench (11.6) while Chig Okonkwo scored 2.2 in the flex. 7-1, a game behind the Fellowship.

Man Getting Pissed at Football had the highest-scoring player of the week and lost. **Joe Burrow completed 38 of 48 passes for 462 yards and five touchdowns**, threw three interceptions, and won the game with a touchdown run in overtime (39.48). Unfortunately, 141 of those yards went to Chase, who plays for the other team. With Christian McCaffrey, Nico Collins and Malik Nabers all on byes, the rest of the lineup was DJ Moore with a touchdown from Josh Allen (14.6), Stefon Diggs with one from Daniels (11.3), Keenan Allen (10.2), and then Isaiah Davis 1.5, Mark Andrews 2.9, Roschon Johnson 3.3 and Jason Myers 1. The best lineup (99.48) loses too. 1-7, tied for last.

## Remix to Commission 102.3 — Ball Like Smush 85.38

I'd like to report that I made the right calls at quarterback and tight end this week. I would also like to report that I didn't make them. Brock Purdy and Juwan Johnson were on byes, so Trevor Lawrence and Dalton Kincaid started by default. Lawrence completed 10 of 14 for 210 yards and three touchdowns before sitting the fourth quarter of a 42–7 win (19.4), and Kincaid caught four passes for 86 yards and a touchdown from Allen (16.6). James Cook scored in that Bills win (17.1), the Commanders defense held Philadelphia to 10 (14), and Rhamondre Stevenson ran for 82 yards (10.5). The decisions I did make myself went worse: Drake London scored 1.3 in Atlanta's shutout loss while Malik Washington caught a touchdown on my bench (12.5), and new tight end Mike Gesicki caught one of Burrow's five touchdowns on the bench (15.6). The best lineup was 129.4. I won by 16.92, I'm 5-3, and I've asked the NFL to schedule more byes for my roster.

Hockenson caught a touchdown from Kyler Murray (13.8), and Rachaad White ran one in for Washington (13.0). Brandon Aubrey kicked three field goals in Dallas's overtime loss (12). Matthew Stafford threw for 157 yards (8.28), and Emanuel Wilson scored zero in Seattle's loss. And one transaction note: Adonai Mitchell, who scored zero in his one start for Ball Like Smush last week, has been released. This week he caught seven passes for **142 yards** and a touchdown for the Jets. 4-4.

## Thats My Commish Do Ya Know Him 129 — The Charleston Kirks 104.26

The biggest margin of the week (24.74). Thats My Commish has a new quarterback, Kyler Murray, who threw two touchdowns in Detroit, one of them to Justin Jefferson (19.9 for Murray, 11.8 for Jefferson). Deshaun Watson, the old quarterback, is off the roster, and his week went like this: he threw for 324 yards and no touchdowns in a 27–6 loss in Pittsburgh, 154 of them on 10 catches by Harold Fannin Jr. (18.4), while the Browns were sacked eight times by the Steelers defense (16), which is also this team's. The Kirks are 2-6. The Jaguars defense scored 21 in that 42–7 rout, Drake Maye threw two touchdowns and ran for a third in Miami (22.46), and Rashee Rice came back from IR with a touchdown (14.8). Derrick Henry scored in Buffalo (13.0), and Wan'Dale Robinson caught a touchdown in Tennessee's overtime shootout loss (12.7). But Omar Cooper Jr., last week's 20-point surprise, scored zero in the same game where Mitchell had 142, Will Reichard scored zero, and the best lineup available (116.06) still loses by 12.94.
