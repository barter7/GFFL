*"Protect the football." — every running backs coach in history, a message that reached Los Angeles about two minutes into overtime on Monday night*

Welcome back one and all to the fifth weekly installment of the GFFL Recap! This week brought a 1.1-point game decided by an overtime fumble, a starting receiver ruled inactive before kickoff, two more name changes, and a quarterback who went from bench crime to best score in the league in seven days. The Fellowship of the Ringless is 5-0. The commissioner is 3-2 and would like it noted that he did *almost* everything right this time. Here's how it went.

## Darn Tuten 160.64 — Remix to Commission 159.54

The closest game of the week by a mile (the next closest was 14.1), with the week's highest score beating its third-highest. It came down to Monday night and one running back.

Darn Tuten had four players score at least 26.9, and none of them was a first-round pick. Jayden Daniels ran eight times for **104 yards and two touchdowns** and threw a two-point conversion in Washington's 27–27 overtime tie with the Giants (32.26). Bucky Irving ran for 165 yards, including a 72-yarder, and caught a touchdown in Tampa's upset of Dallas as a 9.5-point underdog (30.5). Christian Watson caught six passes for 143 yards and two touchdowns from Jordan Love, one of them from 53 yards out (29.3). And ninth-round tight end Isaiah Likely caught 11 balls and two touchdowns from Jameis Winston (26.9). The one dud was the first-rounder, CeeDee Lamb (1.9), in that loss to Tampa. Darn Tuten finished Sunday at 160.64 with nobody left to play, and waited.

Remix to Commission went into Monday night needing **23.41 points from James Cook**, and for most of the night he was getting them. Cook ran 25 times for 111 yards and scored twice in Buffalo's overtime win over the Rams, the second a 5-yard go-ahead touchdown with 1:44 left in regulation. The Rams tied it with eight seconds left. Then, with 8:00 left in overtime, Cook fumbled at the Los Angeles 22. Minus two. Cook finished with 22.3, and Remix to Commission lost by 1.1. Without the fumble, it wins by 0.9.

And yet I can't put this one on Cook, because the margin was sitting on my bench in a Patriots jersey for the second straight week. Rhamondre Stevenson scored in New England's 45–17 rout of the Raiders and put up 16 on the bench while Josh Downs started in the flex and scored 6.2. That swap alone wins by 8.7. Everything else went right. Trevor Lawrence (21.74) threw for 331 yards, including a 27-yard touchdown to Parker Washington on fourth-and-5 with 3:26 left in Jacksonville's 19–17 win over the Eagles; Washington caught both Jaguars touchdowns (27.4); the 49ers defense scored 20 in Seattle; Drake London had 118 yards (15.3). Lawrence over Brock Purdy was right by 5.58, and Juwan Johnson outscored Dalton Kincaid again, 3.6 to 3.2, except this week Johnson was the one starting. Two lineup questions answered correctly, one answered wrong the same way as last week. 3-2.

Footnote: that Jaguars win came on a **57-yard field goal with one second left** by Cam Little, Darn Tuten's kicker.

## Thnks fr-1 th Hpprs 121.68 — Thats My Commish Do Ya Know Him 100.14

First, the paperwork: Kim Kardashian Boutte is now **Thnks fr-1 th Hpprs**, which appears to be a Fall Out Boy song title with the vowels removed and a "-1" added. It's 4-1, so nobody's asking for the vowels back. Jordan Love was 25 of 31 for 367 yards and two touchdowns in Green Bay's 31–7 win over Chicago (25.28). George Pickens had 130 yards and a touchdown in Dallas's loss (23.5), Brock Bowers had 110 and a touchdown in the Raiders' 45–17 loss in New England (19.0), and Jameson Williams caught two passes and turned both into touchdowns (17.7). Ja'Marr Chase cleared the concussion protocol and scored in Miami (16.7). Lamar Jackson is now on IR.

Thats My Commish lost by 21.54 and would have won by 13. **Jordan Addison started at receiver and was ruled inactive before Sunday's game with a hamstring injury.** He scored zero. Justin Jefferson, back from his ankle injury, caught a 15-yard touchdown from Kyler Murray in New Orleans and scored 15.8 on the bench. At tight end, Harold Fannin Jr. started and scored 0.9 while Pat Freiermuth caught two touchdowns from Aaron Rodgers on the bench (19.0). That's **34.8 points left on the bench**, the most in the league. The frustrating part: everything else worked. Mike Evans had 113 yards and a touchdown for the 49ers (22.8), Davante Adams caught two touchdowns on Monday night (19.6), Braelon Allen ran for 116 yards including the 22-yard go-ahead touchdown against Cleveland with 3:07 left (18.1), and Spencer Shrader kicked five field goals in a Colts loss (21). Jeremiyah Love ran 12 times for 22 yards (2.2). 2-3.

## Whistlin' Willy's 98.98 — Slob onmy Doubs (DoubanteAdams) 82.4

The other rename of the week: I AM JACK KERLEY is now **Slob onmy Doubs (DoubanteAdams)**, a name built around Romeo Doubs. Romeo Doubs scored 2.1 points this week. The Patriots scored 45. Davante Adams, the other half of the name, plays for a different team in this league and caught two touchdowns.

Slob onmy Doubs is 0-5, scored the lowest total of the week, and had the win on its bench. Roman Wilson started in the flex and didn't catch a pass (0.0) in a Steelers game where Pittsburgh scored 34. Jaylin Noel, for the second straight week, did his best work on the bench: a 9-yard touchdown from C.J. Stroud in Houston's 26–3 win at Tennessee (12.7). Swap those two, put Terry McLaurin (10.2 on the bench, a week after his inactive start) in for Doubs, and it's 103.2. Willy's scored 98.98. That's the first win of the season, sitting on the bench. Sam LaPorta took a short pass 38 yards for a touchdown (17.3), the Texans defense scored 15, and Jalen Hurts managed 13 in a two-point loss in London.

Whistlin' Willy's is 3-2 thanks largely to Jacoby Brissett, who threw for 292 yards and three touchdowns (24.78), two of them long after Arizona's 45–21 loss to Detroit was decided. Kyren Williams scored the touchdown that tied the Bills with eight seconds left on Monday night (16.6), Javonte Williams scored against Tampa (13.2), and Chris Boswell kicked his way to 13. Blake Corum scored in that same Monday game on the bench (14.7) while Tyler Allgeier started in the flex (2.6), and the Rams defense gave up 37 and scored -1. In a 16.58-point win, nobody minds.

## Immaculate Concepcion 115.92 — Smushed Hopes 101.82

This one was settled on Monday night by one quarterback and one receiver on opposite teams. Matthew Stafford, Smushed Hopes' quarterback, completed 40 of 54 for 383 yards and three touchdowns in the Rams' overtime loss to Buffalo (27.32). **Seventeen of those completions went to Puka Nacua**, who caught 17 of 24 targets for 138 yards and a touchdown (29.2) for Immaculate Concepcion. Every one of them counted twice.

Immaculate Concepcion is 2-3. The Jets defense sacked Deshaun Watson and company **eight times** (18 points), and Trey McBride caught nine passes and a late touchdown (17.1). David Montgomery scored 1.1 and is now week-to-week with a groin injury. And a brief update on the quarterback file we closed last week: Bo Nix threw for 123 yards (7.92), and Tyler Shough, on the bench, threw for 96 yards and two interceptions in New Orleans and scored -0.16. A correct call by 8.08 points. It stays closed.

Smushed Hopes is 2-3, one point short of a better lineup mattering. Emeka Egbuka ran 14 yards for a touchdown in Tampa's win and scored 14.2 on the bench while Carnell Tate started and scored 1.1. The best lineup on the roster was 114.92, which loses by exactly one point. Jaxon Smith-Njigba had 102 yards and a touchdown (20.3), T.J. Hockenson followed last week's 13-for-13 with 1.2, and Ashton Jeanty played through an ankle injury for 9.2.

## The Charleston Kirks 139.06 — Chug City 121.94

Last week the Kirks started Marcus Mariota, and Drake Maye scored 26.16 on the bench. This week they started Drake Maye. **Maye completed 26 of 34 for 374 yards and four touchdowns and ran for a fifth** in New England's 45–17 win over the Raiders: 39.46 points, the highest score in the league. From bench crime to the top of the superlatives in one week. Derrick Henry ran for 102 yards, caught a touchdown and ran in the two-point conversion that tied Atlanta with 40 seconds left in a game Baltimore won in overtime (20.5). MarShawn Lloyd scored twice in Green Bay (16.2), Will Reichard made three field goals (16), and the Vikings defense added 13. The Kirks left 4.4 points on the bench, the fewest in the league. 2-3.

Chug City got a monster game from the player it was scolded for benching two weeks ago. Jaylen Warren started in the flex and scored **33.8**: an 80-yard touchdown catch from Aaron Rodgers and a 50-yard touchdown run, 188 yards from scrimmage in all. Tyler Warren had 83 receiving yards and a rushing touchdown (17.9), and Aaron Jones ran for 113 yards and a touchdown (17.2). Dak Prescott threw for 316 yards but also two interceptions, the second at Tampa's 12-yard line with 1:22 left and Dallas down eight (12.64). Jonathan Taylor ran 15 times for 45 yards (9.7). And the kicker: Trey Smack missed an extra point and two field goals in Green Bay and scored **2**, while Cameron Dicker scored 6 on the bench. Three weeks in a row now. It's a recurring segment. 2-3.

## The Fellowship of the Ringless 159.90 — Man Getting Hit By Football 118.90

The biggest margin of the week (41), and the Fellowship is the league's last unbeaten team at 5-0 with 747.96 points, 48.42 more than anyone else. Jahmyr Gibbs ran 25 times for 151 yards and two touchdowns and added 56 receiving yards in Detroit's 45–21 win in Arizona (35.2). Josh Allen threw for 210 yards, ran for 121 more and scored once each way in the overtime win in Los Angeles (30.5). Garrett Wilson caught seven passes for 104 yards and a touchdown (19.9), Matthew Golden had 123 receiving yards (17.3), and Ka'imi Fairbairn kicked four field goals in Tennessee (17). For the second straight week Jared Goff put up a big number on the Fellowship's bench (28.94, four total touchdowns), and Allen still outscored him by 1.56. Even this team's bench decisions are correct. It is not fun to play.

Man Getting Hit By Football is 1-4, and the cruelest part is the receiver decision. Last week, DJ Moore started and scored 2.2 while Deebo Samuel scored 15.5 on the bench, and the game was lost by 0.12. This week Samuel started and scored 1.5, and Moore scored 0 on the bench. The lesson was learned, and it was the wrong lesson. Otherwise it was a perfectly respectable 118.9: Joe Burrow threw four touchdown passes in Miami (24.6), Malik Nabers caught 11 of 20 targets for 121 yards and the late touchdown that tied Washington (23.6), Jason Myers kicked **five field goals** in Seattle's loss to San Francisco (23), and Christian McCaffrey added 15.8. Wrong opponent.
