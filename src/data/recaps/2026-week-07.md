*"The best is the enemy of the good." — Voltaire, on the subject of a perfect lineup built from an imperfect roster*

Welcome back one and all to the seventh weekly installment of the GFFL Recap! After a week of Monday-night cliffhangers, week 7 had none: every game was decided by at least 16 points, and no bench in the league could have changed a result. The Fellowship of the Ringless became the first team past 1,000 points. A team renamed itself to mock its quarterback, benched him, and watched him have his best game of the season. And for the first time all year, the commissioner set the best lineup his roster allowed. Please hold your applause until you see the score.

## More Like Blo Dix 119.02 — Thats My Commish Do Ya Know Him 84.22

The biggest margin of the week (34.8), and the most self-inflicted win you'll ever see.

Last week this team renamed itself More Like Blo Dix after Bo Nix scored 4.48 on Thursday night and Tyler Shough scored 26.96 on the bench. This week the lineup followed the name: Shough started. Shough completed 30 of 38 passes for 278 yards, a touchdown and an interception in New Orleans' 16–10 loss to Pittsburgh (13.82). Nix, on the bench, threw for 222 yards and a touchdown and ran five times for **75 yards** and another touchdown in Denver's 30–16 win in Arizona: **26.18 points, his best game of the season**, a week after his own team named itself after him. And there's more. Before it was More Like Blo Dix, this team was Immaculate Concepcion. KC Concepcion, a week after losing the naming rights, caught eight passes for 129 yards for Cleveland (19.2), also his best game of the season, also on the bench. The roster's best possible lineup was 150.08; no one left more on a bench than its 31.06.

It still won by 34.8, because everyone else showed up. Trey McBride caught 10 of 13 targets for 111 yards and a touchdown (22.1). Luther Burden III caught all four of his targets on Thursday night for **130 yards** and a touchdown (21.7). Tre Tucker had 103 yards and a touchdown in Las Vegas (18.8), and Puka Nacua caught a touchdown from Matthew Stafford (17.8). The Ravens defense gave up 35 to Cincinnati (-3). More Like Blo Dix is 4-3. In seven weeks, the quarterback it started has outscored the one it benched twice.

Thats My Commish scored 84.22, the lowest total of the week, and its best lineup (89.12) would have lost anyway. Deshaun Watson threw for 308 yards and ran in the go-ahead touchdown with 4:39 left in Cleveland's 27–24 win in Tennessee (18.22), Braelon Allen ran for 104 yards in the Jets' loss to Miami (16.4), and Harold Fannin Jr. caught six passes for 91 yards (10.1). That's where it ends. Justin Jefferson (6.4) and Jordan Addison (3.3) came back from the bye to a Minnesota offense that threw for 150 yards in a 16–10 win over Indianapolis. Tetairoa McMillan scored 6.0 in Carolina's 43–13 loss, and Mike Evans is now on the team's IR. 2-5.

## The Irreversible Binomials 119.76 — Remix to Commission 89.62

Jackson Five (and One) lasted one week. The team is now **The Irreversible Binomials**, which is either a math joke or a threat, and it's 6-1. Jameson Williams caught five passes for 91 yards and **two touchdowns** from Jared Goff in Detroit's 20–17 win over Green Bay (23.6). Quinshon Judkins, a 14th-round keeper, ran for 96 yards and two touchdowns in Tennessee (22.9). Ja'Marr Chase caught one of Joe Burrow's four touchdowns in Baltimore (14.7), George Pickens caught one from Dak Prescott on Monday night (13.7), and Jordan Love threw two touchdowns in Detroit (16.96). The only soft spots were Eddy Pineiro (1) and the Titans defense (0).

And now the commissioner. My best possible lineup this week was 89.62. My actual lineup scored 89.62. After six weeks of this column documenting my bench in detail, I finally got every decision right, and I lost by 30.14. In fairness to me, the bench didn't give me much to get wrong: James Cook, Dalton Kincaid, Trevor Lawrence and Parker Washington were on byes, and DeVonta Smith scored zero. Rhamondre Stevenson, back in the lineup, caught five passes for 93 yards and a touchdown from Drake Maye on Thursday night (20.0). Ollie Gordon II ran 25 times for 103 yards and a touchdown in Miami's 28–25 win over the Jets (16.8), and Drake London caught a touchdown in Atlanta's 29–20 win over San Francisco (16.1). Then: Kenneth Walker III managed 36 yards on 14 carries in Kansas City's 20–3 loss in Seattle (7.7). Brock Purdy threw two interceptions in that loss in Atlanta (12.02), and the 49ers committed four turnovers, which is why the 49ers defense scored 1. Juwan Johnson scored 1.2, and Josh Downs 2.8. No eligible swap on my bench would have gained a single point. That's the problem with a perfect lineup: it doesn't come with better players. 4-3.

## The Fellowship of the Ringless 140.82 — The Charleston Kirks 123.94

The Kirks scored **123.94**, the second-highest total of the week, and lost by 16.88. That's how the Fellowship's season goes.

The Fellowship is 7-0 and has **1,003.58 points**, the first team past 1,000 and 68.32 ahead of anyone else. This week's best player in the league started in the flex. Tony Pollard ran 25 times for 129 yards and **three touchdowns** in Tennessee, the last of them tying Cleveland at 24 with 1:01 left (35.0). The Titans lost on a field goal with three seconds left; Pollard's owner did not mind. Zay Flowers caught eight passes for 137 yards and a touchdown in Baltimore (23.7), Jared Goff threw for 303 yards and two touchdowns with Josh Allen on a bye (18.02), and Jahmyr Gibbs carried 29 times for 116 yards (14.8). D'Andre Swift scored on Thursday night (17.0). The bench had 21.6 more points, including a Makai Lemon touchdown on Monday night (16.2), and the best possible lineup was 162.42. It wasn't needed. It never is.

The Kirks did almost everything right. Drake Maye completed 24 of 30 for 321 yards and two touchdowns and ran for a third in Thursday night's 30–24 loss in Chicago (27.44). Omar Cooper Jr. caught all five of his targets for 115 yards and a touchdown for the Jets (20.0), in a week the Jets' own website warned his targets might shrink with Adonai Mitchell back. Cam Skattebo, last week's zero, ran for 124 yards and a touchdown in the Giants' overtime win in Houston (18.6). Colston Loveland, last week's 15.5 on the bench, started and caught a touchdown (16.4). Will Reichard kicked three field goals in Minnesota's 16–10 win (12), and the Vikings defense added 11. Derrick Henry ran 17 times for 45 yards in Baltimore's 35–13 loss (5.4), and the best lineup available (138.74) would still have lost. Wrong week to have your best week. 2-5.

## Darn Tuten 113.92 — Chug City 97.68

The closest game of the week, which this week means 16.24. With Jayden Daniels on a bye, Darn Tuten started Bryce Young, and Young went 14 of 27 for 163 yards and two interceptions in a 43–13 home loss to Tampa Bay and was pulled after three quarters (5.42). It didn't matter, because the Buccaneer on the other end of that blowout was Bucky Irving, who ran 24 times for 106 yards and two touchdowns (23.5). Amon-Ra St. Brown caught nine passes for 153 yards in Detroit (19.7), Josh Jacobs caught a touchdown from Love (17.6), and Jake Bates won that Lions game with a field goal with one second left (9). And CeeDee Lamb caught **two touchdowns from Dak Prescott on Monday night** in Dallas's 38–21 win in Philadelphia (21.9). Darn Tuten is 6-1.

Chug City's quarterback was Dak Prescott. Prescott threw three touchdowns on Monday night (23.28): two to CeeDee Lamb, who was in the other lineup, and one to George Pickens, who plays for The Irreversible Binomials. Every one of Dak's touchdowns this week scored for somebody else in this league, and two of them scored against him. Jonathan Taylor ran for a touchdown in Indianapolis's loss in Minnesota (18.0), and Jaylen Warren ran for 101 yards in New Orleans (15.2), but Aaron Jones Sr. managed 3.1 in the flex while Devaughn Vele caught a touchdown from Shough on the bench (13.9). The best lineup (112.18) still loses. As for the Dicker Watch: Cameron Dicker was on a bye, and Trey Smack, fresh off his 12-point week, is no longer on the roster. Jason Sanders kicked for Chug City and scored 4. Smack, now on nobody's roster, kicked a tying field goal in Detroit. 2-5.

The best lineup (87.48) still loses. 1-6.
